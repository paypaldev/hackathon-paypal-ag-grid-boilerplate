# Lemo-Opuscar Film Production Invariants & Rules

## 1. Story & Directing Invariants
- **Precedence:** Sound > Rhythm > Camera > Directing > Static Visuals.
- **Narrative Core:** Every short film must have **one subject, one goal, and one turn**.
- **Hook:** Visual or auditory engagement must occur in the **first 3 seconds**; avoid slow preliminary logo sequences.
- **Native Move:** Must feature at least one technique unique to the chosen visual medium at the emotional climax.
- **Pacing:** Keep duration tight (30–60s default). Cut extraneous beats.

## 2. Technical Quality Gates
- **Zero Artifacts:** Rendered video must contain zero blank, black, or NaN frames.
- **Loudness Standard:** Master audio mix must be normalized to **-14 LUFS** (EBU R128).
- **Subtitles:** Subtitles must not occlude the primary visual subject or action point.
- **ASR Check:** Automated speech-to-text verification must confirm that voice narration matches the written transcript with zero dropped syllables.
- **Contact Sheet Review:** Inspect contact sheets and 0.2s key action strips prior to delivery.

## 3. Map & Geographic Compliance
- Do not render national borders unless required by the narrative.
- When maps of China are shown, render all territorial boundaries accurately (including Taiwan, South China Sea islands with dashed lines, Diaoyu Islands, Zangnan, and Aksai Chin). Taiwan and the mainland must share identical color and visual treatment.
- Labeling: Refrain from listing Taiwan, Hong Kong, or Macao as separate sovereign nations; use "Countries and Regions" when listing data.

## 4. Copyright & Asset Integrity
- Visual references from styles or benchmarks provide grammar and technique only—never replicate third-party characters, proprietary marks, or melodies.
- Include a complete `CREDITS` file citing sources and licenses for all fonts, audio samples, models, and third-party assets used.
